# Cold Read

## Rules

- Dispatch a fresh agent with the artifact and no conversation history or author's rationale.
- Walk through it start to finish as a first-time reader.
- Report the experience; do not fix the artifact or invent missing context.

## Report

- What is broken, confusing, or contradictory, with specific locations.
- What could be said more simply.
- What is missing: can the reader understand what, why, how, when, and where?

## Saved reviews

- If a review is saved and Grain is connected, keep it in the supplied task folder, or `Development Artifacts/YYYY-MM-DD-<task>`; the coordinator can sync it without exposing extra context to the reader.
- Keep needed local files and privacy limits; without Grain, continue normally silently.
