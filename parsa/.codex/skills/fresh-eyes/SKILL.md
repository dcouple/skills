# Fresh Eyes

The agent that produced an artifact cannot have fresh eyes. This pass
simulates the real reader, then verifies the simulation with an agent
who genuinely has no context.

## The pass

Read the deliverable as the person who will see it on Monday morning
with groggy, tired eyes and zero context. From that seat, answer three
things:

- what is clear
- what isn't
- what questions would they ask

Then improve the deliverable however you deem fit based on those
answers. The creative freedom is real: restructure, retitle, cut,
front-load — whatever makes it land for that reader.

## The repeat

Once your improvements are applied, hand the deliverable to a
fresh-context agent — one that has seen neither its production nor this
conversation — running this same skill. Repeat until a pass produces no
material change, to a ceiling of three passes; after the third, keep the
last version and say the cap was reached. Two passes is typical, and an
autonomous caller needs the ceiling to be finite.

## Boundaries

- Run this before human review is requested. After handoff — a review
  requested, an awaiting-human-review label, recorded evidence citing
  exact text — propose edits instead of applying them.
- Improve how the facts land, never the facts: claims, numbers, and
  evidence are read-only.
- The persona is the tool. Do not soften it to "review for clarity";
  groggy, Monday, zero context is the standard the deliverable must
  survive.
